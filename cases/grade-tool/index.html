Sport, trad & bouldering grades across every major system
Route grades converted across French, YDS (US), UIAA, British tech/adj (trad), and Ewbank (AU/NZ). Grading systems were developed independently for different climbing styles and regions, so conversions are reliable to roughly half a grade in the middle of the scale, tighter at the extremes.
Boulder grades converted between V-Scale (US) and Fontainebleau/Font (Europe). Like route grades, these are approximate equivalents, not an exact mathematical mapping.
